# Clivi

> Clivi is the leading digital clinic in Mexico for treating diabetes and obesity. It offers personalized medical care combining endocrinologists, nutritionists, and psychologists with continuous glucose monitoring technology and GLP-1 medications. Based in Mexico City, it serves patients nationwide via telehealth. Over 10,000 patients treated, 95% satisfaction rate, and a 4.8-star rating.

Clivi treats type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, and obesity with an integrated approach. Patients receive virtual consultations with specialists, personalized nutrition plans, continuous glucose monitoring, GLP-1 agonist medications (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Saxenda, Rybelsus), and psychological support. Treatment plans include Clivi Pro, Clivi Plus, Clivi Total, and Clivi Zero (focused on weight management). Clivi also operates Farmacia Clivi (farmaciaclivi.com.mx), a digital pharmacy specializing in diabetes and obesity medications.
